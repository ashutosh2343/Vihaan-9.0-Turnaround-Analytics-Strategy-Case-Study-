# Vihaan 9.0 Turnaround Analytics & Strategy Case Study

> A data-driven business transformation case study analyzing the turnaround of Vihaan 9.0 — IEEE DTU's flagship hackathon — through participant analytics, operational strategy, and executive dashboards.

---

## Project Overview

Following the operational challenges and negative participant sentiment surrounding **Vihaan 8.0**, the organizing committee faced reduced stakeholder confidence and uncertainty regarding institutional funding for the next edition.

As the **Event Lead** for Vihaan 9.0, I led the development of a data-driven strategy to evaluate previous shortcomings, redesign participant experience, and communicate the proposed improvements through executive dashboards and strategic presentations.

This project documents the complete analytical process behind that transformation using **SQL, Excel, and Power BI**.

---

## Business Problem

Vihaan 8.0 encountered several operational challenges including:

- Poor participant experience
- Weak hospitality and infrastructure
- Negative online feedback
- Declining stakeholder confidence
- Limited sponsor confidence
- Hesitation from college administration regarding future funding

The objective of this project is to demonstrate how data analytics can support strategic decision-making and operational improvement for large-scale events.

---

## Objectives

- Compare Vihaan 8.0 and Vihaan 9.0 participant datasets
- Analyze participant demographics and diversity
- Evaluate geographic reach and institutional participation
- Develop executive dashboards for stakeholder decision-making
- Identify operational bottlenecks and improvement opportunities
- Document strategic interventions implemented in Vihaan 9.0
- Present a consulting-style business case study of the turnaround
